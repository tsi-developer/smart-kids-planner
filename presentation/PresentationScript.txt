



Hello, my name is tsige, and this is my final project: Smart Kids Daily Planner.

The problem I wanted to solve is that parents need a simple way to organize their children's daily routines.

My solution is a full‑stack web app that lets parents add tasks, manage children, and receive email notifications.

I built the frontend using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The backend is built with Node.js and Express. For email notifications, I used Nodemailer with Mailtrap.

Let me show you the demo. Here is the planner form. Here is the children management screen. And here is the email notification working in Postman.

Some challenges I faced were configuring Mailtrap and connecting the frontend to the backend. But I solved them and now the project works fully.

In conclusion, the project meets all final deliverables: a working frontend, backend email logic, and full documentation.
